# Kaska — Hero Illustration Brief: "The Bid Plane"

**What we're commissioning:** one flagship isometric technical illustration for kaska.ai,
in the genre of Palantir's "Multimodal Data Plane" hero (palantir.com — see reference).
This is the single signature visual of the site. Quality bar is that Palantir asset.

## About Kaska (context for the artist)
Kaska is estimating & contract intelligence for specialty subcontractors (mechanical,
electrical, plumbing, HVAC, concrete…). Software reads a contractor's bid folder
(ITB, takeoff, vendor quotes, subcontract) and prices the job from the contractor's
own completed-job history. Products: **Bidline** (estimating), **Redline** (contract
review), **Cortex** (the model of the contractor's history), **Atlas** (portfolio map).

## The scene
An isometric "plane" (a thin floating slab) labeled **Kaska Cortex** on its front edge.
- **Below / flowing in:** a field of isometric cubes carrying labeled document chips —
  `ITB.pdf`, `Takeoff.bbx`, `Quotes ×12`, `Sub-K v3`, `Fixture schedule`, `Cost codes`,
  `Pay apps`, `Change orders`, `Drawings`, `GC: Vanguard`, `Vendor: Ridgeline`.
- **On the plane:** fine line-art of construction systems being understood —
  a building frame (columns/beams), a pipe run with fittings, a duct run, conduit,
  spotlight circles highlighting details (a fixture, a priced line item as a
  barcode-like glyph). Two floating product tags: **Bidline**, **Redline**.
- **Flowing out (right):** clean output chips — `$5,209,003 · reconciled`,
  `P(win) 34% · EV-optimal`, `2 flags · ranked by risk`, `12 live · 1 drift`.
- Second edge label: `History [+] Model [+] Bid`.

Narrative in one line: *chaotic documents flow onto the Cortex plane and come out
as one defensible bid.*

## Style
- Dark ground `#0B0D12`; near-black cubes with fine light edges.
- Accents: electric blue `#4C7DFF` + violet `#8B7CF6`, used sparingly (glows,
  a few colored cube tops, the pipe run). Pale chips for outputs.
- Fine 1–1.5px linework, isometric (30°), subtle glow on the line-art.
- Labels in a monospace face, small caps chips with hairline borders.
- No people, no photorealism, no gradients-as-decoration. Precision over flash.

## Deliverables
- Master file (Figma or AI) + layered export.
- **SVG** (preferred, optimized) and PNG @2x (~3200px wide), dark background baked.
- A cropped 16:9 variant for social/OG.
- License: full buyout / work-for-hire for web + marketing use.
